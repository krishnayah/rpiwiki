[Web Technologies Group](/docs/rpi-wiki/student-government/student-senate/committees/web-technologies-group/) chairperson, Aaron Niyazov, was inspired by the SLC announcements and decided to also write a poetic announcement message

> Dear **==@Web Technologies Group==**, 'Tis the midst of winter, and soon the sun shall set. The sky will grow dim, our streets dark. The exams pile on, as we gather in our study halls for collective warmth and solidarity in these trying times. The future looks bleak. Good thing we have future webtech meetings to look forward to! Please fill out the scheduler if you're interested in attending!


This was followed up by an announcement by Griffin Oliver '27 for [Academic Affairs Committee](/docs/rpi-wiki/student-government/student-senate/committees/academic-affairs-committee/) 

> Hello everyone. Each day, the sun sets a little later. As winter begins to come to a close, the chance for growth and renewal approaches. The birds are beginning to chirp, and soon the snow will melt. Leaves will return to the trees, and joy will return to our hearts. And yet, when I think of that day I will spend looking out at the sunset, with the spring winds at my back, I can't help but ask; "what did we leave behind?" What I know we did not leave behind, however, is the Academic Affairs Committee, which meets today at 7 in the SGS
